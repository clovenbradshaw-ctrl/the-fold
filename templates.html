surfaces — the nine ways to show data

Every representation has one native terrain — the single cell its structure is built to hold — and the catalog is closed at nine. A spreadsheet, a graph, a legend and an EKG strip are not four interface choices; they are four terrains wearing pixels. Real screens are composites; these are the atoms. Each surface below is built mechanically from data — no model types a figure — and each says what it is blind to, because a surface that cannot name what it misses invites being mistaken for the whole.
